# Privacy Policy — MCMods

**App:** MCMods  
**Developer:** Konselit Studio  
**Android package:** `com.konselit.mcmods`  
**iOS bundle ID:** `com.konselit.mcmods`  
**Effective date:** November 25, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 25, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **MCMods** (the “App”), developed by Konselit Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, and handles information. The App is an unofficial companion that helps users browse, download, favorite, and open Minecraft Bedrock Edition community content such as addons, maps, resource packs, and skins.

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios, Microsoft, or Minecraft.

## 1. At a glance

- No account registration is required.
- The App does not contain advertising SDKs or in-app purchases.
- We collect limited app usage and technical analytics to understand and improve the App.
- Analytics are processed using Google Firebase Analytics and Konselit Studio’s analytics infrastructure, which uses Supabase.
- Favorites, downloads metadata, language, theme, and caches are stored locally on your device.
- We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following information locally:

- Favorite content items
- Downloaded file metadata and related library entries
- Downloaded content files needed to open them in Minecraft
- Language preference
- Theme preference
- Temporary catalog and content caches for faster browsing
- Analytics preference and a randomly generated analytics identifier

These preferences and files are stored using on-device storage (such as AsyncStorage and local file storage). Favorites lists, download libraries, and settings profiles are not uploaded as account data to our analytics backend.

## 3. Analytics information we collect

We use Firebase Analytics and our own analytics infrastructure to measure App usage, diagnose issues, and improve content discovery and navigation. Depending on the platform, configuration, and features used, analytics may include:

- App interaction events, such as session starts, screen views, item views, searches, favorites, downloads, and Minecraft open attempts
- Event timestamps, screen names, content type, item identifiers, file type, and non-sensitive error categories
- App version, operating system, device category or model, and language or locale information
- App-installation, analytics, and session identifiers used to distinguish events or sessions without requiring an account
- Approximate geographic information (such as country or region) derived by analytics providers from technical connection data

Firebase Analytics may also process device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted by device settings and SDK configuration. Our use of analytics is for App measurement and improvement, not to serve personalized advertising. Technical requests to service providers may involve processing IP addresses for service operation and security.

Search analytics record search mode and approximate result-count buckets. We do not intentionally include the raw text of your search queries in analytics events. We do not ask for your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, camera recordings, or microphone recordings. We do not intentionally include your name, email address, or phone number in analytics events.

## 4. Catalog, downloads, and Minecraft handoff

To show available content, the App requests catalog and media information from our online catalog services. When you choose to download an item, the App retrieves the selected file over the network and stores it on your device so you can open it with Minecraft.

Opening content in Minecraft may use your device’s file-sharing / intent mechanisms. Minecraft and any other apps you open are separate products governed by their own privacy policies.

## 5. Why we use this information

- Understand which screens, features, and content types are used
- Measure downloads, open attempts, and related success or error patterns
- Improve performance, usability, personalization-style browsing, and catalog quality
- Maintain the reliability and security of our analytics and catalog services

We do not sell analytics information. The App does not use the collected events to show personalized ads.

## 6. Service providers and data transfers

We use the following service providers for analytics, catalog delivery, and supporting infrastructure:

- **Google Firebase Analytics**: app usage measurement and reporting. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Supabase**: infrastructure used by Konselit Studio to deliver catalog data and to receive, store, and analyze selected App analytics events. See the [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

Data processed through these services may be stored or processed in countries other than your own, subject to the applicable providers’ terms, safeguards, and legal requirements. We use providers to operate our services; we do not sell this information to data brokers.

App distribution platforms, such as Google Play and the Apple App Store, may separately process information under their own policies. If the App embeds or opens third-party media (for example YouTube videos) or external websites, those services’ privacy practices will apply.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Local favorites, downloads, language, theme, and cache data remain on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Analytics events and related technical identifiers may remain in Firebase Analytics and our Supabase-backed systems for as long as reasonably necessary to measure App usage, maintain our services, and meet applicable legal requirements. Analytics data may be deleted or aggregated when no longer needed. Retention within third-party platforms is also subject to their configured retention settings.

Because the App does not require an account, we may not be able to associate every historical analytics event with a particular person. You can contact us at the address below to ask about the data we process or request access to, correction of, or deletion of data where applicable and technically possible. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 8. Your privacy choices

Your device and operating system may offer controls for advertising identifiers and certain data-processing preferences. You can also contact us with questions about analytics collection or to exercise applicable privacy rights. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored favorites, downloads, preferences, and caches from your device.

## 9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal contact information from children. If you believe information about a child has been collected inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, our analytics practices, service providers, or legal requirements. Any changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above.
